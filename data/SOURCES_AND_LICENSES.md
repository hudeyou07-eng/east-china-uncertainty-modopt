# Sources and access conditions

The files in `data/input/` are cleaned derivatives or audit copies supplied within the research project. They are versioned in this private repository so authorized students can reproduce the analysis from the same inputs.

## Required provenance review before public release

| Data family | Current role | Public redistribution decision |
|---|---|---|
| Provincial retail prices | B0 and B1 | Confirm source terms and attribution before public release |
| Regional consumption | Baseline construction | Confirm statistical source terms and attribution |
| Nutrient composition | Constraint coefficients | Confirm database citation and redistribution terms |
| China LCA | Environmental coefficients | Confirm database or publication license |
| Global environmental factors | Sensitivity and boundary checks | Confirm source specific licenses |

Until that review is complete, keep the repository private and limit access to authorized collaborators. A future public release should retain the scripts, schema, manifest and citations even if some data must be distributed through a separate controlled archive.
